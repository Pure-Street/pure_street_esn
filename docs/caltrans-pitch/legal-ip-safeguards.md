# Legal and IP Safeguards for ESN Agency Presentations

Prepared for Pure Street / Environmental Sentinel Network (ESN)

## Important Notice
This document is strategic planning material, not legal advice. Pure Street should consult a California attorney, procurement counsel, and patent attorney before relying on this material for formal legal filings, contract negotiations, or intellectual-property protection.

## Protection Objective
The goal is to present ESN to Caltrans and other agencies convincingly while protecting the parts of the system that create durable competitive advantage.

## What Can Be Public
The following may be safely discussed in public-facing materials:

- ESN as a field intelligence and corridor stewardship platform
- The problem: fragmented corridor visibility, recurring dumping, delayed verification, weak response feedback loops
- General workflow: report, verify, prioritize, coordinate, resolve, measure
- Pilot structure: 90-180 day demonstration, corridor selection, dashboard, evaluation
- Public benefits: cleaner corridors, improved accountability, better coordination, stormwater awareness, accessibility visibility
- High-level dashboard screenshots
- Non-sensitive sample hazard categories
- General privacy and governance commitments
- Planning-level budget ranges

## What Should Stay Protected
Do not publicly disclose the following without attorney review, written protections, or a strategic reason:

- Full scoring formulas
- Weighting logic for severity, recurrence, equity, stormwater, response delay, and verification confidence
- Backend architecture diagrams that reveal implementation details
- Source code
- Database schemas beyond high-level examples
- Proprietary data-cleaning methods
- Full automation workflows
- Detailed procurement strategy
- Vendor lists and cost structure
- Internal pricing floor
- Full roadmap for multi-agency expansion
- Any unique machine-learning, routing, deduplication, forecasting, or audit-integrity methods

## Patent Strategy
Software and civic-tech patents can be difficult, but not impossible, if the invention is framed as a specific technical system or method rather than a broad abstract idea.

Potential patentable areas to discuss with a patent attorney:

1. Corridor environmental hazard verification workflow that combines geotagged evidence, multi-party verification, recurrence detection, and resolution auditing.
2. Environmental health scoring method for transportation-adjacent corridors using hazard type, severity, recency, recurrence, vulnerability overlays, drainage proximity, and response status.
3. Evidence packet generation system for public-right-of-way maintenance that compiles verified field records, jurisdictional routing, map context, before/after media, and resolution status.
4. Partner coordination system for distributing cleanup tasks across agencies, community partners, sponsors, and field verifiers based on risk, geography, and verification confidence.
5. Public accountability dashboard method that separates internal operational data from public aggregated metrics while preserving privacy and impact reporting.

Recommended next step: schedule a provisional patent consultation before presenting highly detailed technical methods to multiple agencies.

## Provisional Patent Filing
A provisional patent application may provide an early filing date while allowing Pure Street to continue developing the invention. It generally lasts 12 months and must be converted into a non-provisional application to continue. A provisional filing is not a granted patent.

Before filing, prepare:

- System title
- Technical problem
- Technical solution
- Diagrams
- Workflow steps
- Data inputs and outputs
- Example use cases
- Variations of the invention
- Screenshots and mockups
- Claims-like descriptions for attorney drafting

## Trade Secret Strategy
Some parts of ESN may be better protected as trade secrets rather than patents.

Potential trade secrets:

- Scoring weights
- Verification confidence model
- Data-cleaning rules
- Recurrence-detection logic
- Agency routing rules
- Internal quality-control procedures
- Pricing strategy
- Partner onboarding playbooks

Trade-secret protection requires reasonable steps to keep the information confidential. Use access controls, private repositories, NDAs where appropriate, and redacted public materials.

## Copyright Strategy
Copyright protects expression, not the underlying idea. It may apply to:

- Written reports
- Slide decks
- Website copy
- dashboard designs to a limited extent
- training manuals
- forms
- diagrams
- brand materials

Recommended action:

- Keep dated versions in GitHub
- Mark documents with copyright notices
- Register key materials with the U.S. Copyright Office when commercially important

Suggested notice:

Copyright © Pure Street. All rights reserved. Prepared for agency briefing and review. No reproduction, redistribution, or derivative use without written permission except as expressly authorized.

## NDA Strategy
Do not require an NDA for the first meeting with a public agency unless they offer one or the conversation becomes technically detailed. Public agencies may resist NDAs due to transparency laws and procurement rules.

Better first-meeting strategy:

- Present high-level concept
- Demonstrate user experience
- Share public-facing pilot proposal
- Withhold proprietary formulas and backend logic
- Offer deeper technical review under a written pilot agreement, evaluation agreement, or confidentiality terms where legally available

## Public Agency Presentation Safeguards
When presenting to Caltrans or other agencies:

1. Use the phrase `proposed pilot` until an agreement is signed.
2. Avoid claiming endorsement, partnership, or approval.
3. Show outcomes and workflows, not proprietary formulas.
4. Share screenshots and sample data, not live sensitive records.
5. Keep internal architecture private.
6. Use versioned PDFs with copyright and disclaimer language.
7. Send follow-up summaries that document what was disclosed.
8. Keep meeting notes.
9. Record attendees and dates.
10. Store all presentation materials in GitHub or controlled cloud folders.

## Anti-Idea-Theft Presentation Doctrine
The goal is to sell the value of the system without giving away the build manual.

Say:

- ESN uses verified field reporting and corridor-level analytics to identify recurring hazards.
- ESN generates evidence packets and impact reports for agency review.
- ESN supports pilot evaluation through defined metrics.

Do not say in public meetings:

- Exact scoring formulas
- Exact data model
- Exact deduplication logic
- Exact automation routes
- Full vendor implementation plan
- Full technical roadmap

## Contract Clauses to Seek
In any MOU, pilot agreement, or services contract, seek language covering:

- Pure Street retains ownership of pre-existing intellectual property
- Agency receives limited use rights for pilot outputs
- Derivative software remains Pure Street property unless specifically negotiated
- Confidential materials remain confidential where legally permissible
- Public communications require mutual review
- Data-sharing obligations are defined
- Termination does not transfer ownership of ESN
- Case studies require approval
- Payment milestones are clear
- Scope changes require written change orders

## Procurement Risk
Avoid giving the agency a complete build specification that could be copied into an RFP for competitors. Instead, provide:

- Outcome requirements
- Pilot success metrics
- Functional categories
- Demonstration screenshots
- Data governance principles

Withhold:

- Implementation recipe
- Proprietary workflow logic
- Internal cost stack
- Full technical architecture
